# Data Attribution

The fixture slice committed to `data/01_raw/fixtures/` is derived from two public
datasets. Both are English-language subsets; see the full dataset cards for
provenance of individual articles and passages.

---

## CC-News (`stanford-oval/ccnews`)

**Source:** Stanford OVAL Lab  
**Hugging Face repository:** <https://huggingface.co/datasets/stanford-oval/ccnews>  
**Pinned commit SHA:** `d733e654c9a506df519e1a166a86c118c7657ce4`  
**Subset used:** First 200 English-language articles from the `train` split  
**Fixture path:** `data/01_raw/fixtures/documents.jsonl` (entries with `id` prefix `ccn-`)

CC-News aggregates news articles from Common Crawl's CC-News dataset.
Individual articles carry their original publisher copyright.
Reproduction is subject to the publisher's terms; this fixture is used
for educational and research purposes only, consistent with the
[Common Crawl Terms of Use](https://commoncrawl.org/terms-of-use/).
